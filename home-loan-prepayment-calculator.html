Home Loan Prepayment Calculator

Enter your outstanding loan details and a one-time prepayment to see two common lender outcomes: (A) keep EMI the same → reduced tenure, or (B) keep tenure the same → reduced EMI. The tool also shows interest saved.

Prepayment Form

What this does

ActionOutcome
Keep EMI sameLoan is repaid sooner (reduced tenure). Calculator shows months/years left and interest saved.
Keep Tenure sameEMI reduces for the remaining tenure. Calculator shows new EMI and interest saved.

Notes

This calculator models standard amortising loans with monthly compounding and monthly EMIs. Actual lender behaviour may differ (prepayment charges, minimum balance rules, interest recalculation dates). Use this as an estimate and confirm with your lender.
