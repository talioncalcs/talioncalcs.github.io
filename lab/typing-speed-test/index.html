What is Typing Speed?

Typing speed is measured in words per minute (WPM). This tool calculates WPM based on the number of correctly typed characters during the test. Correct characters are divided by five (standard word length) and then scaled per minute.

How WPM is calculated here

WPM = (Correct characters ÷ 5) ÷ Minutes. For example, 250 correct characters in 1 minute = (250 ÷ 5) ÷ 1 = 50 WPM.

Tips to improve typing speed

  • Practice daily for short focused sessions.
  • Start slow to build accuracy, then increase speed.
  • Use proper finger placement and posture.
