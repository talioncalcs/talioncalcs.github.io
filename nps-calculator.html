NPS Calculator – National Pension Scheme Returns

Estimate how your contributions to the National Pension Scheme (NPS) can grow over time. Enter your contribution amount, choose contribution frequency, expected annual return and tenure to project the retirement corpus and total invested.

NPS Formula

The future value (corpus) for regular contributions can be derived from the future value of an annuity. This calculator supports both annual and monthly contributions and uses an iterative compounding approach for accurate, step-by-step accumulation.

Formula (annuity/future value reference):

M = P × [ ( (1 + r)t − 1 ) / r ] (for annual contributions)
or iteratively for monthly contributions using r/12 per month.

Where:

  • P = Contribution per period (monthly or annual)
  • r = Annual return in decimal (e.g. 10% = 0.10)
  • t = Tenure in years
  • M = Estimated corpus at the end of tenure

Because NPS contributions can be monthly or annual, this tool compounds month-by-month for monthly contributions and year-by-year for annual contributions to avoid rounding differences and to provide realistic snapshots.

This NPS calculator provides a practical projection of your retirement corpus under the National Pension Scheme by compounding your contributions at an expected annual return. It is intended to help with retirement planning by showing how contribution size, frequency and tenure affect the final corpus.

When to use this calculator

Use this when planning long-term retirement savings under NPS, to compare how contribution frequency or amount alters the final corpus, or to evaluate how much you need to contribute to reach a retirement goal.

How it works (plain explanation)

For monthly contributions the calculator converts the annual return to a monthly rate and iterates month-by-month: each month the contribution is added then monthly interest is applied. For annual contributions it iterates year-by-year. Earlier contributions compound for longer, which is why tenure has an outsized impact on final corpus.

Benefits of NPS investing

NPS offers tax benefits, low management costs, and access to diversified asset classes. It is a long-term retirement vehicle that encourages disciplined saving and can build a substantial corpus over decades when contributions are consistent.

Factors that affect NPS returns

Key drivers are contribution amount, contribution frequency, expected return (which depends on asset allocation and market performance), and tenure. Because NPS is market-linked, actual returns will vary — use conservative estimates for planning.

Frequently asked questions

Is annuity purchase mandatory? A portion of the NPS corpus typically must be used to buy an annuity at retirement as per regulations; this calculator shows the total corpus before annuity purchase.

Can I change contribution frequency? NPS generally allows flexible contributions; confirm specifics with your provider.

Are returns guaranteed? No — NPS returns are market-linked and not guaranteed.
