Income Tax Formula & Slabs (used by this calculator)

This calculator computes Taxable Income as:

Taxable Income = max(0, Gross Annual Income − Total Deductions)

After obtaining taxable income, the calculator applies slab-wise rates depending on the selected regime and age. The slab calculation is performed by summing tax per slab portion:

Tax = Σ (portion_in_slab × slab_rate)

This Income Tax Calculator gives a quick estimate of tax payable under the New and Old regimes commonly used in India. Enter your gross annual income and any deductions or exemptions you expect to claim (for the old regime). Choose the applicable regime and your age group to account for age-based basic exemption limits in the old regime. The calculator performs slab-wise computation and returns taxable income, estimated tax payable and an effective tax rate.

When to use this calculator

Use this tool for a quick comparison between the New and Old tax regimes, to understand how much tax you may owe for a given gross income and deduction amount. It helps with basic planning and comparing the effect of increasing deductions or switching regimes.

How it works (plain explanation)

The calculator subtracts declared deductions from gross income to compute taxable income. It then applies the slab-wise tax rates appropriate to the selected regime. For each slab, only the portion of income that falls into that slab is taxed at that slab's rate; these taxes are summed to produce the tax payable displayed. This mirrors how Indian income tax slabs are applied in practice.

Limitations and what this does not include

This estimate does not include cess (health & education cess), surcharge, special tax treatments (capital gains, business income with presumptive schemes), or exact rebate and credit calculations. It also assumes the user has aggregated and entered all deductions correctly. For official liability and filing, always refer to Income Tax Department guidance and a qualified tax adviser.

Frequently asked questions

Which regime should I choose? That depends on your deductions and exemptions. The New regime offers lower slab rates but limits deductions; the Old regime allows many exemptions (like 80C) which may reduce taxable income if you claim them. Compare both using this calculator.

Does this calculator include cess and surcharge? No — the calculation excludes cess and surcharge and is intended as a quick estimate. Add cess (typically a percentage on tax) and surcharge separately if applicable.

Are the slab rates always the same? Slab rates and thresholds are set by the annual Union Budget and may change. Use conservative planning and consult official announcements for the latest rates.
