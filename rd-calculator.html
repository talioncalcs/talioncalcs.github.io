RD Formula

The maturity value of a recurring deposit is calculated by compounding each monthly installment at the applicable interest rate until maturity.

Formula (mathematical):

M = P × n + P × ( n(n + 1) / 2 ) × ( r / 100 ) × ( 1 / 12 )

Where:

  • P = Monthly deposit amount
  • r = Annual interest rate
  • n = Total number of months
  • M = Estimated maturity value

This calculator uses a month-by-month compounding approach aligned with standard bank RD calculations, ensuring accurate estimation of maturity value and interest earned.

This RD calculator estimates the maturity amount of your recurring deposit by applying interest to each monthly installment for its remaining tenure. It is useful for conservative investors seeking predictable and guaranteed returns over a fixed period.

When to use this calculator

Use this calculator when planning fixed monthly savings through a bank or post office RD. It helps you understand how much your regular deposits will grow and how much interest you will earn by maturity.

How it works (plain explanation)

Each monthly deposit earns interest for a different length of time. Earlier deposits earn interest for longer periods, while later ones earn for fewer months. The calculator adds up the interest earned on all installments to estimate the final maturity value.

Benefits of recurring deposits

Recurring deposits encourage disciplined savings, offer guaranteed returns, and are suitable for short- to medium-term goals. They are low-risk, predictable, and easy to manage.

Factors that affect RD returns

The monthly deposit amount, interest rate, and tenure directly affect RD maturity value. Rates vary by institution and tenure, and premature withdrawals may reduce returns.

Frequently asked questions

What is an RD? A recurring deposit is a monthly savings scheme with fixed returns.

Is RD interest taxable? Yes — RD interest is taxable as per your income tax slab.

Is RD safe? Bank and post office RDs are considered low-risk and secure.
