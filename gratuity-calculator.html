When to use this calculator

Use this tool when you want a quick estimate of gratuity payable on exit or retirement based on your last drawn basic+DA and length of service.

How it works (plain explanation)

The calculator uses completed whole years of service. If both joining and leaving dates are provided, the tool computes completed years between those dates. Otherwise it uses the manual years entry. Gratuity is computed as:

Gratuity = (Last drawn basic + DA) × (15 / 26) × Completed years of service

Notes & limitations

  • This is an estimate. Employer-specific rules, contract terms, and statutory caps or changes are not modelled except an optional cap field.
  • Most schemes use completed whole years — fractional years are not counted by this calculator when using dates.
  • Tax treatment and statutory caps may change; consult your HR or tax adviser for precise entitlement and taxability.

Frequently asked questions

Why 15/26? 15 days' salary for each completed year, with salary expressed on a monthly basis and 26 working days considered for each month yields the 15/26 factor.

If I have odd months, are they counted? This calculator counts only completed whole years. Employers sometimes have rules for rounding — check your policy.
